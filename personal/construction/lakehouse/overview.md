# Home renovation project patterns

Generic checklist for owner-builder style renovations.  
**Public note:** no property addresses, permits tied to a specific site, or identifying detail.

## Typical workstreams

- [ ] Initial cleanout (dust, trash, parts consolidation)
- [ ] Exterior wood repair (soffit/fascia, treatment options)
- [ ] Garage organization / repairs
- [ ] Subfloor repair where damaged
- [ ] Insulation (exterior walls, interior, attic)
- [ ] Electrical (outlets, switches, lights, fans, backup power planning)
- [ ] Exterior living spaces (deck, landscaping)
- [ ] Inspection and permit research for your local jurisdiction
